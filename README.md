# 🎬 My Favorite Movie

## Descendants (2015)

**Genre:** Musical, Fantasy, Adventure

*Descendants* is a Disney Channel movie about the teenage children of famous Disney villains. They are given a chance to leave the Isle of the Lost and attend school in the kingdom of Auradon.

### Why I Like It

I enjoy this movie because of its music, interesting characters, colorful world, and storyline. I also like how it explores whether people should be judged based on their parents or given the opportunity to make their own choices.

- Catchy songs and musical performances
- Interesting characters
- Fantasy setting
- Themes of friendship and identity

**My Rating:** 10/10 ⭐

[Learn more about Descendants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descendants_\(2015_film\))

# 📺 My Favorite Series

## Safe Skies, Archer (2023)

**Genre:** Romance, Drama

*Safe Skies, Archer* is a Filipino romantic drama series about a flight attendant and a pilot whose lives and relationship develop through unexpected circumstances.

### Why I Like It

I enjoy this series because of its romantic storyline, characters, and emotional moments. It is interesting to see how the characters deal with their personal lives and relationships.

- Romantic storyline
- Interesting characters
- Emotional scenes
- Filipino entertainment

**My Rating:** 10/10 ⭐
